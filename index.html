INTERNSHIP: HOUSEKEEPING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

HMS is a simulation-based system developed for College of Business Administration students to help them prepare for their internship and future workplace responsibilities. The system provides a controlled environment where students can practice performing housekeeping-related tasks and become familiar with actual workplace processes before entering a real-world work setting.

The system has two types of users: Administrators, who serve as supervisors responsible for managing the system, monitoring student activities, and overseeing assigned tasks; and Students, who perform specific housekeeping tasks and follow the procedures assigned to them.
